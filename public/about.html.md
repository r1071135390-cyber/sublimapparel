# About SublimApparel — Yiwu Custom Apparel Factory Since 2018

> SublimApparel is a Yiwu-based apparel factory producing custom sublimated, all-over digital printed, DTG, DTF, and screen-printed apparel for B2B customers worldwide. Founded 2018. 12 production lines. 70% in-house. DDP to 100+ countries.

**Last updated:** 2025-08-18 · **Author:** Ramon Wang, Sales Director

## Answer capsule
**We are a Yiwu-based custom apparel factory, not a trading company.** Our facility is in Beiyuan Industrial Park, Yiwu, with 12 production lines and 4 sublimation printers. We produce 70% in-house (cut & sew, printing, finishing, QC, packing) and 30% via specialized partners (embroidery, special-effect decoration, wash). Sales Director: Ramon Wang.

## Supply chain workflow (8 steps)
1. **Inquiry & quote** — 12h response
2. **Artwork preflight & mockup** — 24h
3. **Sample making** — 7–10 days
4. **Sample approval & deposit** — 30% TT
5. **Bulk production** — 25–35 days
6. **Inline QC** — at cutting, printing, sewing, finishing
7. **Final QC & packing** — AQL 2.5
8. **Shipment & tracking** — sea/air/express, DDP

## Quality standards
- **AQL 2.5** on final inspection (industry standard for apparel export)
- **Pre-shipment inspection**: PSI report with photos, measurements, defect log
- **Inline checks**: at cutting, printing, sewing, finishing
- **Fabric inspection**: 4-point system, 10% lot check on bulk
- **Wash test**: 5 cycles per dye lot
