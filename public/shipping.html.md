# Shipping & Fulfillment — DDP Worldwide, 100+ Countries

> We ship DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) to 100+ countries by sea, air, and express. One invoice, no surprise duties or last-mile fees. US warehouse in Fontana, CA and EU rep in Rotterdam, NL provide regional support.

**Last updated:** 2025-08-18 · **Author:** Ramon Wang, Sales Director

## Answer capsule
**DDP means we deliver the goods to your door, with all duties, taxes, and last-mile fees included in the single price we quote you.** We consolidate with a freight partner, file customs on your behalf, and hand off to local last-mile carriers. You receive one invoice, one tracking number, and one delivery date.

## What's included in the DDP price
1. **Production cost** (garment + print)
2. **Export packing** (polybag, hangtag, carton, pallet if needed)
3. **Inland transport** Yiwu → port of export
4. **Export clearance** in China
5. **Ocean / air / express freight** (Incoterms DDP)
6. **Import duties and taxes** in destination country
7. **Customs clearance** in destination country
8. **Last-mile delivery** to your warehouse, store, or event venue
9. **Cargo insurance** (optional, ~0.3% of cargo value)

## What's NOT included
- Unloading at destination if no forklift is available (~$50–$150 surcharge)
- Remote area surcharges (DHL/FedEx define; we notify you upfront)
- Storage beyond 7 free days at destination warehouse

## Shipping modes we offer
- **Sea FCL (20ft / 40ft)** — for 5,000+ pcs, most cost-efficient
- **Sea LCL** — for 200–5,000 pcs, slower, but flexible
- **Air freight** — for 200–2,000 pcs, fast
- **Express (DHL / FedEx / UPS)** — for samples, small orders, dropship
- **Multimodal rail** (China-EU rail) — for 2,000–8,000 pcs to EU, 18–22 days, cheaper than air
