# SublimApparel — Yiwu Custom Sublimate Apparel Factory

> Yiwu-based custom apparel factory delivering true all-over sublimation on polyester, full-body allover digital print on cotton, plus DTG/DTF on cotton, screen print, embroidery, and DDP shipping to 100+ countries.

**Last updated:** 2025-08-18 · **Author:** Ramon Wang, Sales Director · **Reading time:** 5 min

## What we make in one sentence
Custom apparel — T-shirts, hoodies, polos, tank tops, jerseys, pants, shorts, caps, kids' wear, uniforms, workwear — printed edge-to-edge or positionally, then shipped door-to-door with one invoice.

## Core numbers (original data, 2024-2025)
- **8+ years** producing sublimated apparel in Yiwu (founded 2018)
- **70% production in-house** (cut & sew, printing, finishing, QC, packing); 30% specialized subcontracting (embroidery, wash, special trims)
- **12 production lines**, 4 sublimation printers, 2 DTG, 2 DTF
- **MOQ 50 pcs** for sublimation cut & sew
- **50+ countries shipped DDP** in 2024
- **15-25 days** production lead time after sample approval
- **US warehouse in Fontana, CA** (92680) — stock + dropship
- **EU representative** based in Rotterdam, NL

## Print techniques we run
- [Sublimation](https://sublimapparel.com/technique/#sublimation) (polyester, all-over)
- [Allover digital print on cotton](https://sublimapparel.com/technique/#allover-digital-cotton) (true full-body cut-and-sew)
- [DTG](https://sublimapparel.com/technique/#dtg) (cotton, A4–A3 per panel)
- [DTF](https://sublimapparel.com/technique/#dtf) (cotton, polyester, blends)
- [Screen printing](https://sublimapparel.com/technique/#screen) (≥200 pcs)
- [Embroidery](https://sublimapparel.com/technique/#embroidery) (3D puff available)
- [Heat transfer / vinyl](https://sublimapparel.com/technique/#heat-transfer)
- [Cut & sew](https://sublimapparel.com/technique/#cut-sew)
- [Rhinestone, 3D puff, reflective, glow-in-dark](https://sublimapparel.com/technique/) — special-effect decoration
