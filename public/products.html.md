## Customization options per category
- All categories: custom fabric, color, print, sizing, label, hangtag, polybag, carton
- [Sublimation](https://sublimapparel.com/technique/#sublimation): available for T-shirts, hoodies, polos, tank tops, long sleeves, jerseys, pants, shorts (any 100% polyester or poly-blend ≥80%)
- [Allover digital print on cotton](https://sublimapparel.com/technique/#allover-digital-cotton): T-shirts, hoodies, long sleeves, tank tops, kids
- [DTG / DTF](https://sublimapparel.com/technique/#dtg): T-shirts, hoodies, polos, tank tops, kids, accessories
- [Embroidery](https://sublimapparel.com/technique/#embroidery): polos, caps, hoodies, uniform
- [Screen printing](https://sublimapparel.com/technique/#screen): T-shirts, hoodies, polos, bags
